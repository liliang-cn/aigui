Custom blocks: a folder with `aigui.json`, a plain `plugin.js` and a `spec.md` in `~/.config/aigui/plugins` adds a block type to `aigui_guide`, pages, PNGs and standalone exports. `aigui plugin new <name>` scaffolds one; `aigui plugin list` shows what loaded and why anything did not.
