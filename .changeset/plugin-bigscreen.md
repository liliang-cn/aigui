New package: `@ai-gui/plugin-bigscreen` renders ` ```bigscreen ` blocks as animated data walls — KPIs that count up with deltas and sparklines, dial and ring gauges that sweep, rank bars that grow, ECharts charts drawn in the wall's palette, and, with the optional `echarts-gl` peer, 3D bars, scatter, surfaces and lines that turn, plus a globe with arcing routes over a texture painted in-page rather than fetched. Dark and light themes, an accent colour, and a 12-column grid the model lays out.
